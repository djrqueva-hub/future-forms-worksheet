grammar 1 – will / won’t

Form: will + base verb / won’t + base verb

Use: instant decision, promise or offer, prediction

grammar 2 – be going to

Form: am / is / are going to + base verb

Use: plan or intention decided before speaking; prediction based on present evidence

Example: We’re going to plant trees in the school garden.
